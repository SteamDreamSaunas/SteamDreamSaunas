Our Story

Rooted in tradition, built for modern outdoor living

Steam Dream Saunas is focused on creating authentic Finnish barrel and cube saunas made in Lithuania. The goal is simple: to build outdoor wellness spaces that feel timeless, perform reliably and give customers a genuine sauna experience at home.

We believe a sauna is more than just a place to sit in the heat. It is a ritual, a way to relax, and a space to slow down. That is why every sauna is designed with comfort, heat balance and durability in mind, so it feels just as good to use as it looks in the garden.

Whether you are upgrading your backyard or creating a private wellness retreat, Steam Dream Saunas combines traditional sauna values with practical delivery, assembly and customization options to help bring that vision to life.
